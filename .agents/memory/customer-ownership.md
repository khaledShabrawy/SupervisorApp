---
name: Customer ownership
description: Customer visibility is company-wide rather than restricted to an individual supervisor.
---

The user states: “customers belong to a company, not to a specific supervisor.”

**Why:** Applying supervisor ownership to customer reads incorrectly hides company customers from non-admin users.

**How to apply:** Keep customer lists, searches, and cards restricted to the user's company, not to the customer's supervisor identifier. This visibility rule does not expand mutation permissions or remove supervisor restrictions from visit records.
